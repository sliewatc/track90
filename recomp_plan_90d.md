# 90-Day Body Recomposition & Aesthetic Plan

- **Stats:** 6'1", 185 lbs *(34.0" starting waist placeholder—update in Progress)*
- **Current Lifts:** Bench Press (~135 lbs, newly consistent), Squat (~225 lbs), Deadlift (~315 lbs), Pull-Ups (<5 strict)
- **Schedule & Lift Window:** Flexible rolling schedule (lift when motivated, 4-day base + optional 5th day), typically **after work / evening (5:00–8:00 PM)**.
- **Gym Equipment Notes:** Smallest barbell plates are **5 lbs** (`+10 lb` total barbell jumps); small dumbbells and cables support **2.5 lb** jumps.
- **Primary Goal:** Body recomposition—simultaneously reducing body fat while building upper-body aesthetics (upper chest, capped side/rear delts, arms, back width, visible abs) and overall strength over 90 days.

- **Sleep Target:** 7–8 hours per night (non-negotiable for preserving lean muscle and strength progression in a deficit).
- **Session Time Cap:** Every workout is structured to finish in **40–55 minutes** (strictly under 1 hour, including warm-up and commercial-gym transitions).

## Part 1: Nutrition & Calorie-Cycling Protocol

### Weekly Calorie & Macro Targets (Averaging ~2,330–2,400 kcal/day)

- **Lifting Days (4–5 days/week): 2,500–2,550 kcal**
  - **Protein:** 180–185g | **Fats:** 65–70g | **Carbs:** 285–305g
- **Rest Days (2–3 days/week): 2,050–2,100 kcal**
  - **Protein:** 180–185g | **Fats:** 65–70g | **Carbs:** 175–195g
  - *Hunger-Management Option:* If rest-day hunger spikes after heavy lower-body sessions, tighten the swing to **2,450 kcal lifting / 2,200 kcal rest** for the same weekly deficit.

### Execution & Appetite-Control Rules

- **4-Meal Protein Bolus Schedule (180–185g total):** Split protein into 4 feedings spaced 3–4 hours apart, with the Pre-Sleep Anchor counting as **Meal 4** (not an extra 5th meal):
  - **Meal 1 & Meal 2 (Before 1:00 PM):** Front-load **75–85g protein** (~40–45g each) and **12–15g fiber** with moderate carbs.
  - **Meal 3 (Post-Workout / Dinner, 5–8 PM Window):** **55–65g protein** + **60–70% of daily carbs** around evening training.
  - **Meal 4 (Pre-Sleep Protein Anchor):** **35–40g** of casein protein, cottage cheese, or thick Greek yogurt 60–90 minutes before bed to protect lean muscle overnight.
- **Daily Fiber & Hydration:** Aim for **30–35g total fiber** and **3–4L water/day** (especially when taking creatine and electrolytes).
- **High-Protein Takeout Playbook (Weekday Go-Tos):**
  - *Rule of Thumb:* Double lean meat (chicken breast, steak, shrimp, grilled fish), ask for **sauces/dressings on the side**, and load fibrous veggies first.
  - *Chipotle / Bowl Spots (~620–720 kcal | 55–65g P):* Double chicken or steak, light/half rice (full rice on lifting evenings), fajita veggies, black beans, salsa/pico; skip queso & heavy sour cream.
  - *Sweetgreen / Cava Salad & Grain Bowls (~550–650 kcal | 45–55g P):* Double grilled/harissa chicken, greens + half grains, roasted veggies, light dressing on the side.
  - *Panda Express / Asian Grill (~580–680 kcal | 48–55g P):* Double Grilled Teriyaki Chicken (sauce on side) or String Bean Chicken Breast over steamed veggies (or half white rice on lifting evenings).
  - *Poke / Sushi (~550–650 kcal | 45–50g P):* Double salmon/tuna poke over greens + half rice (or sashimi + edamame + 1 simple roll); easy on spicy mayo/crispy onions.
- **Holiday & Restaurant Playbook (3-Plate Rule + Calorie Banking):**
  - **Calorie Banking:** Trim **150–200 kcal** of carbs/fats on the 2–3 days before Thanksgiving, Christmas, or a big dinner out to bank a guilt-free **+450–600 kcal buffer** while keeping your weekly average on track.
  - Fill half the plate with lean protein first (turkey breast, roast beef, shrimp, salmon).
  - Fill the remaining half with fibrous vegetables/salad before touching starches or desserts.
  - Cap alcohol at **1–2 drinks max** (clear spirits with soda water or dry wine) and skip sugary cocktails.

### Sample High-Protein Home Shortcuts

- **Greek Yogurt Berry Bowl (~430 kcal | 45g Protein | 5 min prep):** 1.5 cups plain nonfat Greek yogurt mixed with 1 scoop whey, mixed berries, sliced almonds, and light honey.
- **Chicken Quinoa Power Bowl (~560 kcal | 52g Protein | 25 min prep):** 6 oz grilled chicken breast over microwave quinoa, spinach, cherry tomatoes, cucumber, avocado, and lemon.
- **Post-Workout Beef or Chicken Rice Bowl (~680 kcal | 50g Protein | 85g Carbs | 10 min prep):** 6 oz 93/7 ground beef or chicken breast over 1.5 cups jasmine rice with light soy/sriracha + banana/pineapple.
- **Sheet-Pan Teriyaki Salmon & Broccoli (~590 kcal | 44g Protein | 20 min bake):** 6 oz wild salmon + broccoli florets with light soy-ginger glaze, roasted at 400°F (200°C) for 14–16 min.
- **Pre-Sleep Cottage Cheese or Casein Bowl — Meal 4 (~280 kcal | 38g Protein | 3 min prep):** 1.5 cups low-fat cottage cheese (or 1.5 scoops casein mixed thick) with cinnamon and berries.

### Evidence-Based Support (Optional)

- **Creatine Monohydrate:** 5g daily (expect a normal 2–4 lb water-weight bump in Weeks 1–2).
- **Caffeine:** 100–200mg earlier in the day (**strictly before 2:00 PM**). For **5–8 PM evening workouts**, use **electrolytes only (non-stim)** so sleep stays protected at 7–8 hours.

## Part 2: Training Execution Rules

- **2-Minute Shoulder Prehab (Before Upper Days):** Perform **1–2 light sets of Cable or Band External Rotations (12–15 reps/side)** + **Band Pull-Aparts (15 reps)** before pressing to warm up and protect your right rotator cuff.
- **Unilateral Left-First Rule (Imbalance Fix):** On every dumbbell and single-arm cable exercise, **always start with your LEFT (weaker) arm** and stop your right arm at the exact same rep count.
- **5-Minute Compound Ramp-Up Protocol:** Before working sets on your first heavy compound each day (Bench, Squat, DB OHP, Deadlift), run a crisp ramp-up: **Empty bar/light × 10 → 50% × 5 → 75% × 3 → 90% × 1** (60–90 sec rest between ramp-up sets).
- **Double Progression & Logbook Tracking:** Log every set (`weight × reps + RIR`). Pick a weight where you hit the bottom of the rep range with **1–2 reps in reserve (RIR)**. Keep that weight until you hit the top of the rep range across all sets, then progress:
  - **Barbells (Bench, Squat, Deadlift, Barbell RDL):** Since your gym's smallest barbell plate is **5 lbs**, progress by **+10 lbs** total (+5 lb/side) once you hit the top of the rep range across all sets.
  - **Non-Barbell Compounds (DB OHP, DB Rows, Pulldowns, Leg Press):** Add **5 lbs** next session.
  - **Small Isolations (Lateral Raises, Curls, Pushdowns, Rear Delts):** Add **2.5 lbs** (or expand the rep range by 2–3 reps before jumping 5 lbs).
- **Intensifiers (Phase 2 Only — Weeks 8–13):** Run Phase 1 (Weeks 1–6) as clean straight sets at 1–2 RIR. In Phase 2, layer in **Lengthened-Partial Finishers** (3–4 bottom half-reps past failure on Incline DB Press, Cable Lateral Raise, Seated Leg Curl, OH Cable Tricep Ext) and **Rest-Pause Finisher Sets** (failure, rest 15s, 3–5 more reps on DB Lateral Raise, Leg Extension, Cable Pushdown).
- **Commercial-Gym Supersets & Flexibility:** Keep compounds as straight sets; superset late-session isolations (`A1/A2`, `B1/B2`). Run the 4-day base on a **flexible rolling schedule** (add the Optional 5th Day whenever motivated & recovered).

## Part 3: 4-Day Upper/Lower Base Split (All Under 1 Hour)

### Upper A (~52–55 min | Strength, Chest, Back & Arms)
- **Flat Barbell Bench Press:** 3 × 5–8 *(2.5–3 min rest — primary anchor lift)*
- **Single-Arm Dumbbell Row** *(left arm first)*: 3 × 8–10 *(90–120s rest | Swaps: Chest-Supported Row, Seated Cable Row, Machine Row)*
- **Incline Dumbbell Press (30° bench):** 3 × 8–12 *(90s rest | Phase 2: lengthened partials | Swaps: Smith Incline, Machine Incline)*
- **Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown:** 3 × 10–12 *(90s rest | Swaps: Assisted Pull-Up, Neutral-Grip Pulldown)*
- **Superset A1 — Single-Arm Cable Lateral Raise** *(left arm first)*: 3 × 12–15 *(60s rest | Swap: DB Lateral Raise)*
- **Superset A2 — Cable Face Pull:** 3 × 12–15 *(60s rest | Swaps: Reverse Pec Deck, Rear-Delt DB Fly)*
- **Superset B1 — Cable Tricep Pushdown** *(No skullcrushers)*: 3 × 10–12 *(60s rest | Swap: Single-Arm Cross-Body)*
- **Superset B2 — Dumbbell Hammer Curl** *(left arm first)*: 3 × 10–12 *(60s rest | Swap: Rope Cable Hammer Curl)*

### Lower A (~50–52 min | Squat, Quads & Weighted Abs)
- **Barbell Back Squat:** 3 × 5–8 *(2.5–3 min rest | Swaps: 45° Leg Press, Hack Squat, Smith Squat)*
- **Romanian Deadlift (BB or DB):** 3 × 8–10 *(2 min rest | Swaps: 45° Weighted Back Extension, Cable Pull-Through)*
- **45° Leg Press (feet low):** 3 × 8–12 *(90–120s rest | Swaps: Hack Squat, Smith Squat, DB Bulgarian Split Squat)*
- **Seated Leg Curl:** 3 × 10–12 *(75–90s rest | Phase 2: lengthened partials | Swap: Lying Leg Curl)*
- **Superset A1 — Standing Calf Raise:** 3 × 12–15 *(60s rest | Swaps: Leg-Press Calf Press, Seated Calf Raise)*
- **Superset A2 — Hanging Leg Raise:** 3 × 10–15 *(60s rest | Swap: Captain's Chair Raise)*

### Upper B (~50–53 min | Pull-Ups, Shoulders, Upper Chest & Arms)
- **Strict Pull-Ups (AMRAP Test Set):** 1 × max strict reps *(stop at 1–2 RIR; 2 min rest — tracks progress toward 8–10 strict)*
- **Seated Dumbbell Overhead Press (30° elbows):** 3 × 6–8 *(2–2.5 min rest | Swaps: Machine Shoulder Press, 60° High-Incline DB Press)*
- **Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown:** 3 × 8–10 *(2 min rest | Swaps: Assisted Pull-Up Machine, Wide-Grip Pulldown)*
- **Smith Machine Incline Press (30°):** 3 × 8–10 *(90–120s rest | Swaps: Barbell Incline, Converging Incline Machine)*
- **Wide-Grip Seated Cable Row:** 3 × 8–10 *(90s rest | Swaps: 30° Incline DB Row, Chest-Supported Machine Row)*
- **Superset A1 — Dumbbell Lateral Raise:** 3 × 12–15 *(60s rest | Phase 2: rest-pause)*
- **Superset A2 — Reverse Pec Deck:** 3 × 12–15 *(60s rest | Swaps: Cable Face Pull, Rear-Delt DB Fly)*
- **Superset B1 — Incline Dumbbell Curl** *(left arm first)*: 3 × 10–12 *(60s rest | Swap: Bayesian Cable Curl)*
- **Superset B2 — Overhead Cable Tricep Extension** *(No skullcrushers)*: 3 × 10–12 *(60s rest | Phase 2: lengthened partials | Swap: Single-Arm OH DB Ext)*

### Lower B (~54 min incl. cardio | Posterior Chain, Core & Conditioning)
- **Conventional Deadlift:** 3 × 5 *(2.5–3 min rest | Swaps: Trap-Bar DL, BB/DB RDL 3×8)*
- **Leg Extension:** 3 × 12–15 *(75s rest | Phase 2: rest-pause | Swap: Walking DB Lunges)*
- **Lying Leg Curl:** 3 × 10–12 *(75s rest | Swap: Seated Leg Curl)*
- **Superset A1 — Standing or Leg-Press Calf Raise:** 3 × 12–15 *(60s rest | Swap: Smith Calf Raise)*
- **Superset A2 — Kneeling Cable Crunch:** 3 × 10–15 *(60s rest | Swaps: Ab Crunch Machine, Decline Weighted Crunch)*
- **Conditioning Finisher:** 15 min incline treadmill walk (10% @ 3.2 mph), or swap for 2 × 8–10/leg DB Bulgarian Split Squats.
