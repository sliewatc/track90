# Track90 Project Rules

## Design & Visual Innovation Philosophy

- **Novelty over status quo:** Entirely novel, unconventional, and completely different visual appearances and interaction models than status-quo fitness apps or standard web/SaaS templates are explicitly encouraged when they make the UI clearer, faster, or more satisfying to use.
- **No timid defaults:** Do not default to generic card stacks, conventional form layouts, or safe category conventions when a radically different spatial hierarchy, custom visual instrument, or new control paradigm better serves a lifter on the gym floor.
- **Design for the real scene:** Every novel layout or interaction must remain unmistakably legible at arm's length on mobile iOS Safari (390×844 portrait PWA), fast one-handed with sweaty thumbs, accessible (`>= 4.5:1` contrast, real `<button>` targets `>= 44px`), and smooth at 60fps (`transform`/`opacity`/`clip-path` only; preserve the GPU WAAPI FLIP accordion).
- **Preserve product truth:** Bold visual and interaction reinvention should never weaken data accuracy, offline localStorage persistence, backup/restore compatibility, or engine calculations.
